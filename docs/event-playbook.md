# Casa Alternativa Event #0 Playbook

## Format

Event #0 is a small Jikanle evening in Bogota: chill conversation base, shared playlist, and one *cancion de la noche*. The first song is **Fuyu no Hanashi** by Centimillimental.

## Timing

- 5:00-6:00 PM: arrival, names, languages, and light conversation.
- 6:00-7:00 PM: guided Fuyu no Hanashi lesson using the slide deck.
- 7:00-8:00 PM: second listen with lyrics, then free talk around the prompts.

## Materials

- Fuyu no Hanashi slide deck.
- Luma event link: `https://luma.com/Jikanle?k=c`.
- Name stickers grouped by target language.
- Small printed vocabulary sheet.
- Host phone with the Android app installed for screenshots and tester onboarding.

## Post-Event Flow

- Export attendee emails from Luma.
- Invite attendees to Play Console closed testing.
- Invite attendees to Room #0.
- Ask everyone to share a short "historia de invierno" in the room.
- Use the next song suggestion from what people actually discuss.
